Model self-checks

Reading the model. Installs grow monthly and are amplified by the viral factor (÷(1−K)). Free users retain along a D30-anchored decay curve; a share starts the 7-day trial (mostly Day-0), and trials convert at the trial→paid rate. Annual/monthly mix applies to each paid cohort; the launch-window promo prices the first year of annual cohorts, which renew at full list. Ads monetize free DAU only (Pro is ad-free). AI cost per user is tracked as a margin check, not netted from revenue. Fees: Apple Small Business 15% · Google Play ≈15% effective (10% service + 5% billing, post-June-2026 US) · Stripe 2.9% + 30¢ + 0.5% Billing · RevenueCat 1% of tracked revenue past $2.5K MTR.
